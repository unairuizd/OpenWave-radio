OpenWave - art of the six retro themes
======================================

All the art of the Radio de válvulas, Casete portátil, Boombox, Mesa de DJ, MP3 and
Clásico de rueda themes (cabinets, dials, needles, knobs, magic eye, keys, grilles,
cassettes, reels, speakers, meters, pads, platters, screens, wheels and every other
shape in their layouts) is original work drawn for the OpenWave course project. It is
drawn as vector shapes in XAML and C# (Resources/Themes/ and Views/Controls/) and takes
every colour from the theme palettes.

It is not copied or derived from the design, logo or name of any real radio, cassette
player, music player, DJ equipment or brand, and it uses no third-party glyphs or images.

The themes also reuse the OpenWave icons in Resources/Images (see Icons-LICENSE.txt).

It may be used under the same terms as the rest of the OpenWave source code.
