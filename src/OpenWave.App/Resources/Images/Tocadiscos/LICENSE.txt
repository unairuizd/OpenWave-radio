OpenWave - Tocadiscos theme art
===============================

All the art of the Tocadiscos theme (record with grooves, shine, label and spindle,
tonearm, record sleeves, the small "peeking" disc, the record crate with its front,
partition and slots, and the wooden strips) is original work drawn for the OpenWave
course project. It is drawn as vector shapes in XAML (Views/Controls/ and
Resources/Themes/TocadiscosLayout.xaml) and takes every colour from the theme palette.

It is not copied or derived from the design, logo or name of any real turntable,
record label or brand, and it uses no third-party glyphs or images.

The theme also reuses the OpenWave icons in Resources/Images (see Icons-LICENSE.txt).

It may be used under the same terms as the rest of the OpenWave source code.
